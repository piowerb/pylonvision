What you'll be able to do

From reading your first chart to trading with a written plan

The course moves in order: how markets work, then reading charts, candlesticks and patterns, then indicators, then risk management and psychology, then your trading plan and day or swing trading strategies.

  • ✓Understand how stock, forex and crypto markets work and set up your trading environment
  • ✓Read charts using support and resistance, volume, timeframes and Japanese candlesticks
  • ✓Recognize candlestick and chart patterns, and measure targets from breakouts
  • ✓Use moving averages, MACD, RSI, stochastics, Bollinger Bands, ATR and Fibonacci without cluttering your chart
  • ✓Manage risk with the 1% rule, position sizing, stop-loss placement and risk-to-reward ratios
  • ✓Build a written trading plan and journal, and apply day trading and swing trading strategies

Trading stocks, forex and crypto carries a high risk of loss. This course is educational and is not financial or investment advice.
